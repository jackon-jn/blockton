Blockton

Blockton is a town with strange rules. Every episode opens on one of them ("In this town...") and follows the one resident who doesn't fit. Short animated stories, posted on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

The episodes are written, voiced and animated with AI tools and published by the channel owner through the official APIs of each platform, using a private tool called Blockton Uploader. The tool only ever signs in to the channel owner's own accounts.

Contact: open an issue on GitHub. Last updated 21 November 2026.
